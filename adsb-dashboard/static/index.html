Quick Audit Playbook

  • Hardware Base: If Throttled triggers a status change from OK, your power supply has dropped below 4.63V. Inspect the micro-USB cable immediately to prevent a core kernel crash.
  • Sky Noise: Baseline background static should register between -25.0 dB and -35.0 dB. Readings climbing toward -15.0 dB or higher signal heavy local electromagnetic interference from nearby electronics or cell towers.
  • Filter Check: If noise climbs past -20.0 dB but message throughput remains stable within your standard 45 msg/s to 175 msg/s traffic spectrum, your inline SAW filter is successfully isolating the 1090 MHz band from out-of-band signals.

Chart Diagnostics

  • Signal Accuracy: The Positioning Ratio measures coordinate resolution. Flawless outdoor setups achieve ≥75%. Structural obstacles restrict indoor window deployments to a standard operational range of 40% to 60% due to signal attenuation.
  • Orange Markers: Dashed vertical lines indicate an automatic software gain adjustment. The system dynamically steps the tuner up or down across a hardware range of 0.0 dB to 49.6 dB based on traffic volume.
  • Line Split: Tracks the gap between the bright blue line (Total Heard) and dim line (Positioned). A normal window gap sits at 40% to 60%. If the lines diverge and the ratio drops below 25%, physical obstructions (like blinds or shifted antenna placement) are blocking complex coordinate packets while allowing basic radio pings through.

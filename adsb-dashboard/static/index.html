Quick Audit Playbook

  • Hardware Base: If Throttled triggers, check your micro-USB voltage supply.
  • Sky Noise: Noise above -20 dB signals high urban interference or low cloud cover (ducting).
  • Filter Check: If noise is high but message rates hold steady, the SAW filter is successfully protecting the tuner.

Chart Diagnostics

  • Clean Signal: A high Positioning Ratio (≥75%) means pings are crisp enough to decode full coordinates.
  • Orange Markers: Dashed vertical lines indicate an adaptive gain change.
  • Line Split: If the total messages and bad messages lines pull far apart, the receiver is battling heavy signal distortion.
